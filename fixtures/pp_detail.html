While other listings in the complex boast about the standard lock-up-and-go lifestyle, this specific unit elevates modern living by completely removing the headaches of power cuts and water outages.

This apartment is a rare find, seamlessly blending high-end contemporary finishes with off-grid reliability, all set against a beautiful, leafy backdrop.

The Interior Experience

From the moment you step inside, the open-plan layout feels exceptionally bright and welcoming, grounded by sleek white tiling and sophisticated charcoal feature walls.

Gourmet Kitchen: Features high-gloss white cabinetry, sleek stone countertops, a stunning marble-look splashback, and under-counter space for your appliances.
